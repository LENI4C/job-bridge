The Role

Vanguard Partners is seeking a world-class Executive Assistant to support our Founding Partner & CEO. This is a high-impact role requiring exceptional judgment, poise, and the ability to operate as a strategic extension of the executive office. You will be responsible for managing a highly dynamic environment where precision and discretion are paramount.

Key Responsibilities

  • Orchestrate intricate domestic and international travel itineraries, including private aviation and security protocols.
  • Serve as a primary gatekeeper, managing high-volume communication with portfolio CEOs and limited partners.
  • Lead the coordination of quarterly board meetings and annual investor summits.
  • Execute special projects ranging from real estate acquisition support to personal brand management for the CEO.
  • Maintain absolute confidentiality regarding sensitive firm and personal data.

Requirements

  • Minimum 8 years of experience supporting C-Suite executives at a top-tier financial or legal firm.
  • Bachelor's degree from a leading institution; advanced degree or EA certification preferred.
  • Expert-level proficiency in G-Suite, Microsoft Office, and modern project management tools (Asana, Notion).
  • Ability to remain accessible for urgent matters outside of standard business hours.
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills with a refined professional presence.
